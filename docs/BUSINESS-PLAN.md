# Business Plan — Design-Led Desk Mat Brand

*The proof report. Compiled 13 July 2026. Every number is sourced or its arithmetic is shown; where a figure could not be verified against a primary source, it is marked ⚠ with what to do about it.*

## 1. Market demand evidence

- **Amazon UK** maintains a dedicated best-seller category for desk pads ([Desk Pads & Blotters best sellers](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Best-Sellers-Desk-Pads-Blotters/zgbs/officeproduct/200845031)). Top gaming desk pads show **4,000–8,000+ "bought in past month"** badges. ⚠ Amazon blocks automated access, so these figures come from recent search-indexed snapshots of that page — spot-check the live page before quoting them anywhere public.
- **Market size:** the global mouse pad / desk mat market is estimated at **~$6.2bn (2025)**, with growth attributed to WFH and esports ([Data Insights Market](https://www.datainsightsmarket.com/reports/mouse-pad-and-desk-mat-1311182)).
- **Etsy** has established buyer markets for [desk mats](https://www.etsy.com/market/desk_mat) and [gaming desk mats](https://www.etsy.com/market/gaming_desk_mat) — meaning buyers arrive via Etsy search with purchase intent, no audience required.
- **eBay UK** has a dedicated [Desk Mat category](https://www.ebay.co.uk/b/Desk-Mat/62048/bn_55195893). Sold-listing volumes require Terapeak (free inside an eBay seller account) — pull these during week 1.
- **Content demand:** the [#deskmat tag](https://www.tiktok.com/tag/deskmat) and desk-setup ASMR formats are established on TikTok; a KBDFans "Substance" deskmat video reached **~4M views in a month**; small studios (e.g. Cozyleigh Studios) report going viral with a single video and selling out ([TikTok desk mats discover page](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/desk-mats)). Desk-aesthetics content grew strongly through 2025 ([Hexcal trends](https://www.hexcal.com/blogs/articles/desk-setups-2025-2026-trends-and-predictions)).
- **The branding premium (the core of the business case):** near-identical manufactured goods sell at wildly different prices purely on design/brand:
  - Generic Amazon mats (YSAGi et al.): £8–15
  - Indie design brands (Etsy/TikTok): £25–35
  - Premium brands: Satechi $30–40, Orbitkey $50–90, Oakywood $60–80, [Grovemade $80–170](https://grovemade.com/)
  - Manufacturing cost of a custom-printed mat: **$0.24–$2.88** (§3)

### Validation phase — print-on-demand, £24.99 retail

- Printful desk mat base price: **£7.50** (verified live 13 Jul 2026: [product page](https://www.printful.com/uk/custom/mouse-pads/personalized/desk-mat)). ⚠ On-page size options and UK shipping cost don't render publicly — confirm exact dimensions and shipping in the Printful dashboard before setting final prices; their 36×18" gaming pad analogue typically prices higher (~£16–20).
- Illustrative Etsy sale at £24.99: Printful base + UK shipping ≈ £11.50 → fees ≈ £2.95 (6.5% + 4% + £0.20 + £0.16) → gross ≈ **£10.54 (42%)**.
- 20% Printful discount at 25+ units improves this once designs start repeating.

The validation phase is not the profit engine — it exists to prove which designs deserve a £300–500 white-label order, at zero inventory risk.

## 6. Risk register

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Design-IP discipline** — deskmat culture skews anime/game fan-art; one licensed-character mat recreates the trademark trauma | High | Hard rule: 100% original artwork (abstract, typographic, streetwear-influenced). Osume and indie Etsy studios prove original art sells. Run every design name through the BRAND.md clearance checklist |
| **Commodity undertow** — Amazon/eBay generic segment is a £8–15 price war | High | Stay design-led on Etsy/TikTok/own store; enter Amazon last, as a brand, never compete on price |
| **TikTok Smart Promotions auto-enrolment** (up to 3.5–4.5% GMV) | Medium | Disable on day one |
| **Restock lead times** (~3–4 weeks China production + freight) | Medium | Hold 2×100 units from month 6; reorder at 40% stock remaining |
| **Fee drift** — TikTok's subsidised era already ended (Jan 2026); further rises possible | Medium | Multi-channel by design; own store is the hedge (67% margin) |
| **Single-person dependency** — content stops, sales slow | Medium | Batch-record content; automations (PLAYBOOK §6) keep fulfilment hands-off |
| **⚠ Unverified numbers** — freight £2.20/unit is an estimate; Amazon purchase badges are snapshots | Low | Get real freight quotes at Trade Assurance checkout before committing to batch economics; re-verify Amazon data in week 1 |

## 7. Compliance checklist (GB sale of desk mats)

Lighter than apparel — no fibre-content labelling (rubber-backed mats fall outside the Textile Products (Labelling and Fibre Composition) Regulations) and no UKCA/CE (non-electrical). What *does* apply:

1. **[General Product Safety Regulations 2005](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/general-product-safety-regulations-2005/general-product-safety-regulations-2005-great-britain)** — product must be safe; add traceability marking: brand name, UK contact address, batch/design reference on the product or packaging.
2. **UK REACH** — before the bulk order, obtain the supplier's declaration/test report covering phthalates, azo dyes and PAHs in the rubber and inks ([guidance](https://www.compliancegate.com/uk-general-product-safety-regulations-2005/)).
3. **Selling to NI/EU triggers [EU GPSR 2023/988](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/general-product-safety-regulations-northern-ireland/eu-regulation-2023988-on-general-product-safety-detailed-guidance)** — requires an EU/NI Responsible Economic Operator. **Decision: restrict listings to GB initially**; appoint an REO service (~£100–300/yr) only when EU demand justifies it.
4. **Distance selling** — standard 14-day return right; publish a returns policy on every channel.
5. **HMRC** — register as self-employed (or set up a company) once trading; the £1,000 trading allowance covers only the very first trickle.
6. **Marketplace policy** — Etsy allows third-party manufacturing when the seller is the genuine designer and the production partner is disclosed on the listing ("designed by a seller"; tightened June 2025 to require genuinely original design — which this is). ⚠ Etsy blocks automated access to its policy pages ([help article](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000336547-Working-with-Production-Partners-on-Etsy), corroborated by [this summary](https://listybox.com/blog/etsy-print-on-demand-rules-policies-disclosure-2026)) — read the live policy when opening the shop and set the production partner field on every POD listing.
