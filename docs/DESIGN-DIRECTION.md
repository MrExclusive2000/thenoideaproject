# Design Direction — Collection 01: "After Hours"

*The complete specification for the 10-design validation sprint (PLAYBOOK §2). Each design here can be produced or commissioned without further decisions. Collection concept: the hours people actually sit at their setups — dusk to 3am. Every design is quiet enough to live under a keyboard and strong enough to carry a product photo.*

> **The hard IP rule, restated:** 100% original artwork. No game references, no anime characters, no film palettes-with-a-wink, no fonts without commercial licences (Space Grotesk and Inter are OFL — safe). If a design "reminds you of" something protected, it ships only after redesigning until it doesn't. AI-assisted drafts never ship raw (PLAYBOOK §7).

## Shared print spec (applies to all 10)

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Master artboard | **940 × 440 mm @ 300 DPI, sRGB** (oversized master; crops to any vendor canvas) |
| Target product | 900 × 400 × 4 mm stitched-edge mat |
| Bleed | Design to full 940 × 440 edge — stitching + cut consume the margin |
| Safe zone | Keep names/focal marks ≥ 15 mm inside the 900 × 400 crop |
| Composition zones | Keyboard occupies centre-left; mouse sweeps right third. Put focal detail **top edge and right third**, keep the mouse zone low-contrast for sensor reliability, avoid critical detail in the bottom 80 mm (wrist/palm zone) |
| Brand mark | Monogram (BRAND §2), 12 mm, bottom-right corner, 20 mm inset, tonal (10–15% lighter/darker than ground) — never loud |
| File naming | `dl-<nn>-<design>-cw<n>-print-940x440-300dpi.tif` (+ `-web.jpg` 2000px for listings) |
| ⚠ Vendor canvases | **Verify Printful's exact desk-mat canvas in the dashboard before upload** (their listed product may differ from 900×400 — the £7.50 base price page doesn't expose dimensions; see BUSINESS-PLAN §2). Re-crop the master per vendor template; same for the Alibaba supplier's template at white-label stage |

## Palette governance

Brand surfaces stay strictly on the BRAND §2 palette. Artwork may extend it — this collection deliberately adds `#5E8F6E` (platform green), `#C97A8A` (dusty rose), `#8A8794` (signal grey), `#2E3450`/`#232238` (indigo steps), `#B9B4C7` (chalk line) as **artwork-only** colours. They never appear on packaging, store UI, or social templates.

## The data loop (what graduates a design)

Per PLAYBOOK §3 — a design earns a 100-unit white-label batch when it hits **≥15–20 POD sales or a sustained content spike with sell-through**, margin holds at £29–35, and it fits the brand wall. Track per-design: Etsy views→favourites→orders, TikTok saves/shares per video featuring it, and which *colourway* converts (cw data decides the batch variant). Kill designs with <3 sales and no engagement signal after 6 weeks; replace from the same family as the winners.

## Production notes

- Build each design as vector (SVG/AI) wherever possible; rasterise to the 940×440 master at export. Gradients+grain (06, 07) go raster from the start at 300 DPI.
- Proof every design on both a dark and light desk photo template before listing; the listing's first image is the styled setup shot (MARKETING §2.3).
- Order Printful samples of the 2–3 strongest before launch week for photography and QC (PLAYBOOK §2.4).
