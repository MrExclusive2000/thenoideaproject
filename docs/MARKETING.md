# Marketing — The No-Ads Traffic Engine

*Zero paid ads, zero cold outreach. Three compounding channels: Etsy search, TikTok organic + Shop affiliates, and (later) the own-store repeat loop. All of it is work you already know how to do from the clothing brand.*

## 1. Content pillars (TikTok first; cut-downs to Reels/Shorts)

Proven formats in this exact niche — a KBDFans deskmat video did ~4M views in a month, and desk-setup ASMR is an established genre ([#deskmat](https://www.tiktok.com/tag/deskmat)):

| Pillar | Format | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **Setup ASMR** | Slow unroll of the mat, keyboard placed, cable run, final reveal — satisfying sounds, no talking | 2×/week |
| **Design process** | Screen-record the artwork evolving; voiceover the story ("this one's based on 2am motorway lights") | 1×/week |
| **Unboxing/packaging** | The tube, the tissue, the seal — designed to be filmed (PLAYBOOK §5) | 1×/week |
| **Founder story** | "My clothing brand died over a trademark. Round two." — honest, episodic; people follow comebacks | 2–4×/month |
| **Community setups** | Repost customers' desk photos (ask permission; UGC compounds) | as it comes |

Rules: 3–5 posts/week minimum through months 1–6; every video tags the TikTok Shop product; batch-record on one day per week.

## 2. Etsy SEO (buyers with purchase intent, day one)

1. Use EverBee/eRank (free tiers) to pull volume for long-tail terms: "XL desk mat", "stitched edge desk mat", "minimalist desk mat", "Japanese desk mat", "desk mat 900x400".
2. Title formula: `[Style] Desk Mat XL | [Design name] | Stitched Edge Gaming Desk Pad 900x400 | Desk Setup Accessory`.
3. All 13 tags used on every listing; first photo square-cropped on the mat in a styled setup (your product-photography skill is a real edge here — most competitors use bare supplier renders).
4. 10+ listings live fast — Etsy's algorithm favours shops with browsing depth; colourway variants count.
5. Reviews: follow up every delivered order via Etsy messages with a genuine thanks + care tip; never incentivise reviews (policy violation).

## 3. TikTok Shop Affiliate — the leverage without outreach

TikTok Shop UK's **[Affiliate Open Collaboration](https://business.tiktokshop.com/uk/affiliate)** lets you set a public commission (10–15%) that ANY UK creator can claim by making videos featuring your product. Creators browse the open-plan catalogue and pick products themselves — **they come to you**. UK case studies show affiliate-driven growth without founder outreach ([Mylee £390k; "More or Less" £11k in 2 months](https://socialtale.co/blog/tiktok-shop-case-studies)).

Setup (Seller Center → Affiliate):
1. Open plan at **15% commission** during launch (drop to 10% once velocity exists).
2. Write the affiliate product card like a creator brief: what the mat is, the content angle that works (ASMR/setup), your best-performing video linked as an example.
3. When a creator's video sells, send a free mat for their next video (reactive gifting to people already selling for you is not cold outreach).
4. Margin note: affiliate sales net £17.59 − 15% commission ≈ £13.24 gross — still 46%, and every affiliate video is also a permanent ad you didn't make.

## 5. Channel sequencing (when to add eBay and Amazon)

- **eBay UK — month 4+:** business account, list winners + clear residual POD validation stock; expect price-sensitive buyers; fine at 55% margin, don't chase the £8-mat crowd.
- **Amazon UK — only after the trademark registers** (~4–6 months post-filing): Brand Registry requires a registered mark, and without it Amazon is a generic knife-fight. With it, you get brand-gated listings, A+ content, and the storefront. Enter as a brand at £29–35, FBM first.
- **Own store — month 5–7** (see `STORE-SETUP.md`): the QR insert in every package points here; email list + 67% margin repeat purchases are the long-game asset.

## 6. Review velocity & social proof

- Etsy: aim 15–20 reviews by month 4 (follow-up messages; flawless packaging does most of the work).
- TikTok Shop: shop rating gates affiliate interest — refund fast, never argue in comments.
- Screenshot every customer setup photo (with permission) into a "wall of desks" — future landing-page social proof.
