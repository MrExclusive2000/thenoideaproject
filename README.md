# The No-Idea Project → The Desk Mat Brand

This repo documents the research, decision-making, and complete business blueprint for a **design-led desk mat brand**: original-artwork XL desk mats sold into gaming / desk-setup culture, validated with print-on-demand, scaled with white-label manufacturing, and marketed with zero paid ads and zero cold outreach.

The self-hosted webstore component runs as a Docker container, deployable as a **Pelican Panel egg**.

## The one-paragraph pitch

Desk mats are the centrepiece of every desk-setup photo and one of the few products where a **generic $0.30–$3 manufactured item retails at £25–£170 purely on design and branding**. This plan starts at £0 stock risk (print-on-demand), proves which original designs sell using Etsy search traffic and TikTok organic content, then white-labels the winners at ~£5 landed cost for ~60% margins. All artwork is original — the trademark and licensing lessons from the previous venture are built into the process, including clearing the brand name against trademark databases *before* anything is printed.
